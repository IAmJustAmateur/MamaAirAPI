MamaAir

Privacy Policy

Draft — subject to update

This is a working draft for review. It describes confirmed parts of MamaAir's data processing and is not yet a complete privacy policy.

Draft last updated: 31 October 2026

MamaAir helps you understand air pollution exposure during pregnancy and provides personalised information and recommendations. This draft covers the MamaAir app and its supporting service.

Information you provide

  • Account and profile: email address, name, profile photo, language, country and time zone, where provided.
  • Pregnancy and health: date of birth, height, pre-pregnancy weight, ethnicity, pregnancy history and stage, symptoms, lifestyle and wellbeing information, where provided.
  • App preferences and activity: tracking and notification preferences, consent records, check-ins and completed recommendations or tasks.

Location and environmental information

When movement tracking is enabled and location data is submitted by the app, MamaAir processes location coordinates and timestamps to estimate your exposure to air pollution. The service combines this information with air-quality and weather data to build your exposure history.

Information generated by the service

MamaAir generates exposure estimates, personalised risk indicators, health insights and recommendations using your profile, reported information and environmental data. These generated records form part of your personal information.

How information is used

The service uses this information to operate your account, provide personalised app features, calculate exposure and risk indicators, display your history and recommendations, and process account-related requests such as email verification, password reset and account deletion.

External services

Where Google or Firebase sign-in is used, MamaAir processes authentication identifiers and information needed to verify your sign-in. MamaAir also uses OpenWeather for air-quality and weather information; coordinates are sent when requesting location-based environmental data.

Technical and diagnostic information

The service processes technical information such as IP addresses, client information and request diagnostics to operate and troubleshoot the service.

Your choices and account deletion

You can manage the location permission for MamaAir in your device settings. You can also request account deletion through the public Delete your account page, which guides you through verification and confirmation.

This draft will be updated as the remaining privacy information is confirmed.
