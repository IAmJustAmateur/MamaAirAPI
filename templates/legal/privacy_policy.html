1. About this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how MamaAir collects, uses, stores, protects and shares information when you use the MamaAir mobile application, its supporting services, and its public account-deletion page.

MamaAir is a pregnancy wellbeing and environmental-information service. It provides personalised information about pregnancy, everyday wellbeing, air pollution, weather and environmental exposure.

In this Privacy Policy, “MamaAir,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the developer and operator of the MamaAir application.

  • Website: https://mamaair.africa
  • Privacy Policy: https://api.mamaair.work/privacy-policy
  • Account deletion: https://api.mamaair.work/delete-account
  • Privacy contact: service@mamaair.work

2. Information we collect

The information processed by MamaAir depends on the features you use and the choices you make.

Account and authentication information

We may process:

  • Your name and email address;
  • Your encrypted or securely hashed sign-in credentials;
  • Authentication identifiers and account identifiers;
  • Access tokens, refresh tokens and active-session information;
  • Email verification and password-reset records;
  • Information provided by Google or Firebase when you choose one of those sign-in methods, such as your email address, display name, account identifier and profile image URL.

MamaAir does not receive your Google account password.

Profile and pregnancy information

Where you choose to provide it, we may process:

  • Date of birth;
  • Height and pre-pregnancy weight;
  • Expected due date or current pregnancy week;
  • Pregnancy stage and previous pregnancy count;
  • Country, area, language and time zone;
  • Ethnicity, where this option is available and you choose to provide it;
  • A profile image.

Images selected directly from your device camera or photo library remain on your device unless the app clearly informs you that an upload feature has been introduced. A profile image supplied through a sign-in provider may remain hosted by that provider.

Wellbeing and lifestyle information

Where you choose to use the relevant features, MamaAir may process:

  • Sleep and daily activity information;
  • Diet and hydration information;
  • Work type, cooking method and ventilation information;
  • Time spent in different environments;
  • Mood, feelings and wellbeing check-ins;
  • Physical changes that you choose to record;
  • Daily Plan responses and completed actions;
  • Reminder and notification preferences.

Some of this information may be considered sensitive personal information under applicable privacy laws.

Location and movement information

If you enable location tracking, MamaAir may process:

  • Precise or approximate location coordinates;
  • Location timestamps;
  • Location accuracy and movement information;
  • Indoor or outdoor classifications;
  • Movement history submitted to MamaAir;
  • Environmental exposure records associated with a location.

Location tracking is optional. MamaAir asks for location permission before collecting location information.

When tracking is active, location may be processed while the app is not visible on screen. On supported devices, a persistent notification is displayed while the location service is active.

Turning off tracking stops future collection. It does not automatically remove information that was previously uploaded. Previously uploaded information can be removed by deleting your MamaAir account.

App activity and preferences

We may process:

  • Consent and permission choices;
  • Completed recommendations and Daily Plan actions;
  • Check-in dates and progress;
  • Reminder schedules and notification preferences;
  • App language and appearance preferences;
  • Limited product-interaction events, such as opening a weekly report or completing an action;
  • Account-deletion requests.

The current app does not use a third-party advertising analytics SDK. Limited product-interaction events may be stored in encrypted storage on the device for app functionality and progress management.

Information generated by MamaAir

MamaAir may generate:

  • Indoor or outdoor classifications;
  • Air-pollution and environmental exposure estimates;
  • Personalised environmental indicators;
  • Daily Plans and recommendations;
  • Progress summaries and weekly reports;
  • Pregnancy-stage summaries;
  • Recommendation-completion records.

These generated records may be linked to your MamaAir account and are treated as personal information.

Technical and security information

When you connect to MamaAir, our servers may automatically process limited technical information, including:

  • IP address;
  • Request date and time;
  • Request identifier;
  • App version and operating-system information;
  • Browser or client type;
  • Response status and technical error information;
  • Security events needed to prevent misuse, unauthorised access or attacks.

MamaAir application logs are designed not to contain passwords, authentication tokens, precise coordinates, or sensitive pregnancy and wellbeing entries.

Public account-deletion page

When you use the public account-deletion page, we process the information needed to verify that you own the account, such as:

  • Your account email address;
  • Your password or a sign-in-provider verification token;
  • Essential security cookies;
  • Limited request and security information.

Credentials submitted through the deletion page are used only to verify the request and are not stored as deletion-form content.

3. How we use information

MamaAir uses personal information to:

  • Create, authenticate and protect your account;
  • Verify your email address and process password-reset requests;
  • Provide pregnancy wellbeing and environmental features;
  • Personalise Daily Plans, information and recommendations;
  • Estimate environmental exposure;
  • Display current and historical air, weather and exposure information;
  • Save your preferences, progress and completed actions;
  • Schedule the notifications and reminders you request;
  • Maintain sessions and synchronise information across supported devices;
  • Process account-deletion and privacy requests;
  • Detect, investigate and prevent security incidents or misuse;
  • Troubleshoot technical problems and maintain service reliability;
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

MamaAir does not use personal information to make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

4. Location processing and environmental services

Location tracking is used to provide local environmental information, estimate exposure, and help determine whether recorded activity occurred indoors or outdoors.

Coordinates are sent securely to MamaAir servers. MamaAir servers may provide coordinates to environmental-information providers where needed to complete a location-based request.

MamaAir uses:

  • HERE Technologies for reverse geocoding and location classification support;
  • OpenWeather for location-based air-quality and weather information.

HERE and OpenWeather receive only the information required to return the requested result. MamaAir does not include your name, email address or MamaAir password in these environmental requests.

MamaAir server logs must not record precise coordinates submitted for these features.

5. Permissions and device features

Depending on the features you choose, MamaAir may request access to:

  • Location: for local environmental information and optional movement tracking;
  • Notifications: for reminders, Daily Plan notifications and tracking-status notifications;
  • Camera or photo library: when you choose a profile image.

You can refuse or withdraw optional permissions through the app or your device settings. Some features may not work without their related permissions.

6. Legal grounds for processing

Depending on where you live, MamaAir processes information on one or more of the following grounds:

  • To provide the service you requested;
  • With your consent, including for optional location tracking;
  • To maintain security and service reliability;
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations;
  • For legitimate operational interests that do not override your privacy rights.

Where processing depends on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect processing that occurred before consent was withdrawn.

7. Service providers and information sharing

MamaAir does not sell personal information.

We may provide limited information to service providers that help operate MamaAir, including:

  • Cloud hosting and infrastructure providers;
  • Email delivery providers used for verification and password reset;
  • Google and Firebase, when you choose their sign-in services;
  • HERE Technologies;
  • OpenWeather;
  • Security, maintenance and technical-support providers.

These providers may process information only to provide the relevant service or as required by law.

We may also disclose information:

  • When required by a valid legal request or applicable law;
  • To protect users, MamaAir, or others from fraud, abuse, security threats or unlawful activity;
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing or transfer of the service, subject to appropriate notice and safeguards;
  • When you specifically direct or consent to the disclosure.

MamaAir does not share precise location, pregnancy information or wellbeing entries with advertising networks.

8. Advertising and promotional content

MamaAir may display promotional content selected by the app or by general app context.

MamaAir does not use precise location, pregnancy information or wellbeing entries to personalise advertisements. The current version does not include a third-party advertising SDK and does not provide personal information to advertising networks for cross-app tracking.

9. Local device storage

MamaAir stores some information on your device to keep you signed in and provide app functionality. This may include:

  • Authentication credentials in Android Keystore or iOS Keychain;
  • Profile and preference information;
  • Recommendation and progress information;
  • Notification settings;
  • Limited product-interaction events;
  • Temporarily queued location points.

Sensitive local app storage is encrypted. Device backup and transfer are disabled or restricted for sensitive MamaAir storage.

Signing out clears account-linked queued location information, pending local product-interaction events and scheduled notifications. Deleting the app normally removes its app-managed local information, but uninstalling the app does not by itself delete information previously uploaded to MamaAir servers.

10. Data retention

MamaAir retains information only for as long as it is needed for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

The following retention rules apply:

  • Account and profile information: retained while your MamaAir account remains active and deleted when the account is deleted;
  • Pregnancy, lifestyle and wellbeing information: retained while needed to provide account features and deleted with the account;
  • Recommendations, progress, check-ins and generated records: retained while the account is active and deleted with the account;
  • Server-side movement and environmental exposure history: retained while required to display account history and deleted with the account;
  • Queued location points on the device: retained for no more than seven days and removed earlier after successful upload, sign-out or account deletion;
  • Authentication tokens and sessions: retained until they expire, are revoked, you sign out, or the account is deleted;
  • Email verification and password-reset records: retained until used or expired, and then automatically removed;
  • Operational and security logs: retained for no more than 30 days and then automatically deleted or irreversibly anonymised;
  • Account-deletion request information: retained only for the time required to verify and complete the request, and no longer than 30 days unless retention is required by law;
  • Encrypted backups: rotated and overwritten within 30 days. Deleted account information is not restored to the active service. If a backup must be restored for disaster recovery, completed deletion requests are reapplied.

If MamaAir must retain limited information to comply with a legal obligation, resolve a dispute or prevent fraud, we retain only the minimum information required, keep it separate from normal app use and delete it when the applicable requirement ends.

Aggregated information that can no longer reasonably identify an individual may be retained for service measurement and planning.

11. Account deletion

You can permanently delete your MamaAir account:

  • From the account or application settings inside MamaAir; or
  • Through the public Delete your account page without reinstalling the app.

You may need to verify ownership of the account before deletion. Verification is used to protect accounts from unauthorised deletion.

After a verified deletion request is confirmed, MamaAir:

  • Permanently deletes the MamaAir account;
  • Deletes the associated profile and pregnancy information;
  • Deletes wellbeing, lifestyle and check-in information;
  • Deletes movement, location and environmental exposure records;
  • Deletes reminder, progress, task and recommendation records;
  • Removes authentication information controlled by MamaAir;
  • Revokes active MamaAir sessions and tokens;
  • Clears account-linked data from the app when deletion is initiated in the app.

Deleting your MamaAir account does not delete your separate Google or Firebase account. Those accounts are controlled by their respective providers.

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

12. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Ask whether MamaAir processes information about you;
  • Access or receive a copy of your information;
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • Request deletion of your account and associated information;
  • Restrict or object to certain processing;
  • Withdraw consent;
  • Request information in a portable format;
  • Submit a complaint to an applicable data-protection authority.

You can update available profile information in the app, turn off location tracking in Privacy Settings, and manage permissions through your device settings.

To submit another privacy request, contact service@mamaair.work. We may request limited information to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Please do not include passwords, precise coordinates or sensitive personal information in an email.

13. Users under 18

MamaAir is intended only for people aged 18 or older.

The service performs age validation and does not accept invalid or future birth dates. Users under 18 are not permitted to create or use a MamaAir account.

If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we will disable the account and delete its associated information. Concerns about an underage account can be reported to service@mamaair.work.

14. Security

MamaAir uses technical and organisational safeguards intended to protect personal information, including:

  • HTTPS encryption for information transmitted between the app and servers;
  • Encrypted sensitive storage on supported devices;
  • Android Keystore and iOS Keychain protection for authentication credentials and encryption keys;
  • Access controls and restricted administrative access;
  • Session and token revocation;
  • Data minimisation;
  • Automated retention and deletion controls;
  • Logging rules that exclude passwords, tokens, precise coordinates and sensitive pregnancy and wellbeing entries;
  • Monitoring and maintenance intended to prevent unauthorised access.

No electronic service can guarantee absolute security. If you believe your MamaAir account or information may have been compromised, contact us promptly.

15. International processing

MamaAir and its service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live.

Where applicable law requires additional safeguards for an international transfer, MamaAir uses appropriate contractual, organisational or technical measures to protect the transferred information.

16. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when MamaAir features, service providers, legal requirements or data practices change.

The updated version will be published at the same public URL and its “Last updated” date will be changed. If a change materially affects how sensitive personal information is used, we will provide additional notice or request consent where required.
